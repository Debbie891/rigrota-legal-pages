Contact RigRota

For help with your account, a technical issue, invoices, certificates or your schedule, email support@rigrota.co.uk.

Email RigRota Support

What to include

Tell us which device and app version you’re using, what you were trying to do, and any error message. Please do not email passwords, payment card details, private certificate files or other sensitive records unless a secure method has been agreed.

Connectivity and offline access

RigRota can display previously saved schedules when internet connectivity is limited. If the app reports a sync issue, reconnect when possible and check the latest record status before editing or exporting information.

Privacy and account deletion

For privacy questions, contact support@rigrota.co.uk. To request account and personal data deletion, visit Account Deletion Requests.
