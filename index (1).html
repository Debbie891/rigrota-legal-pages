Who is responsible

RigRota is operated by Robbie Vasey, a sole trader trading as RigRota. For privacy questions or to exercise your data rights, contact support@rigrota.co.uk.

Information we handle

  • Account: email address, sign-in details, profile and account identifiers.
  • Rotations and earnings: rotation schedules, shift records, rates, allowances, overtime and estimated income.
  • Invoices: business and client information, invoice descriptions, totals and PDF documents that you create.
  • Certificates and attachments: document names, dates, private files and images you choose to upload.
  • App and device data: local/offline records, notification preferences, technical errors and feedback you submit.
  • Subscriptions: if paid plans are introduced, the applicable app store and subscription provider may process purchase or entitlement information.

How and why we use it

We use this information to provide and secure the account and requested app features, calculate and display schedules and estimates, sync your data, create invoices, manage private documents, respond to enquiries and maintain service security.

Depending on the activity, processing may be necessary to provide the service you request, comply with legal obligations, pursue legitimate interests such as security and support, or rely on your consent where it is required. Optional device permissions can be controlled in your device settings.

Service providers and sharing

RigRota uses cloud-hosted infrastructure for sign-in, data storage and private document storage. Sign-in providers, email delivery services, and Apple or Google device services may process relevant information when you choose those features. If subscriptions become available, payment and entitlement providers may receive purchase information. We do not make uploaded certificates publicly accessible merely to support sharing.

Information is shared with service providers only as needed to operate the app or meet legal requirements. To request the current provider list and relevant international transfer details, contact our privacy email.

Storage and security

Records may be stored in the RigRota cloud service and in account-scoped storage on your device for offline access. We use access restrictions and private file storage to protect information. No system can guarantee absolute security. Please protect your account credentials and device.

Retention and account deletion

We retain account and service information while reasonably needed to operate RigRota or meet applicable legal requirements. You may request account and associated personal data deletion via our Account Deletion page.

Invoices, payment records and other financial information that RigRota is legally required to keep for UK tax purposes may be retained for the statutory period, generally at least five years after the relevant 31 January Self Assessment filing deadline, or longer where legally required. This does not mean that every user-created invoice must be retained as RigRota’s own tax record. Other personal information should be deleted following a valid request unless an applicable legal exception requires retention. Retained information is restricted to the relevant purpose.

Removing the app from a phone does not itself delete your cloud account.

Your rights

Subject to applicable UK data protection law, you may ask for access to your information, correction, erasure, restriction, portability or object to certain processing. Contact support@rigrota.co.uk. We may need to verify your identity. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

Updates

We may update this policy when RigRota’s services, providers or applicable requirements change. A revised date will be shown on this page.
