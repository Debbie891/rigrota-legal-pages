Account Deletion Requests

How to request removal of your RigRota account and associated personal information.

Request deletion

You may request deletion of your RigRota account and personal data by emailing support@rigrota.co.uk. Please use the email address linked to your RigRota account where possible. We may ask you to complete additional secure verification before processing. Do not send your password.

Email an Account Deletion Request

What happens next

  1. We acknowledge your request and securely verify that you control the account.
  2. We assess what can be erased and what, if anything, must be retained under applicable law.
  3. We arrange deletion of the relevant account records and private files through an authorised process, and explain any exceptions.
  4. We inform you of the outcome and any further action needed.

We normally respond to UK data rights requests within one month, subject to permitted extensions and verification requirements. An email request does not instantly delete your account.

Information covered by deletion

Deletion requests may cover your profile and login access, rotation and shift records, pay settings, uploaded certificates, private files, client and invoice information, feedback and other associated personal data, subject to verification and any applicable legal obligations. Local copies on other devices may also need to be cleared by signing out or removing the app.

Financial records that may be kept

RigRota may need to retain invoices, payment records and other financial information it is legally required to keep for UK tax purposes for at least five years after the relevant 31 January Self Assessment deadline, or longer if legally required. Only information necessary for a valid retention obligation should be retained and access restricted. Not all user-created invoices necessarily fall within RigRota’s own accounting obligations.

Subscriptions

Deleting an account does not automatically cancel store billing. If you have an active subscription, cancel it separately through your Apple account, Google Play subscriptions or applicable web payment account. You can contact support for guidance.

Important note

RigRota’s in-app automated account deletion is not currently enabled. This page provides the external email-based request route. Secure identity verification and account/file deletion must be completed through an approved process; please do not interpret this page as confirmation that any deletion has already occurred.
